Common questions

Straight answers to what electricians ask us

My iPhone already transcribes voicemails

iPhone transcription only works if someone leaves a message. Most callers don't. Our system captures the caller's number and details before they decide to hang up — and texts you within 60 seconds while they're still thinking about you.

I can just see who called and ring them back

You can — but you're calling back blind with no idea what they need, two hours later. Our system tells you the job type, urgency and postcode before you dial. You call back fast and informed. That's the difference between winning and losing the job.

We already have someone answering calls

Does she cover Saturday evenings? Emergency electrical work doesn't keep office hours. Our system works 24/7, alongside whoever you already have. It's not a replacement — it's cover for everything that falls through the gaps.

Seems expensive just to receive contact details

One missed rewire at £2,000 pays for six months of this service. You're not paying for contact details. You're paying to stop losing jobs you're already losing every week.

I'm not interested in AI

Fair enough — forget we said AI. Think of it as an automated answerphone that captures job details and texts them to you instantly. Nothing complicated. No app to learn. You forward your calls and we do everything else.

Do I have to change my number?

No. You keep your existing number completely. You set up a simple call divert — when your phone is busy or unanswered, calls forward to us. Your van livery, website and Google listing stay unchanged.
